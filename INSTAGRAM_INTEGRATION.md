## Where Instagram Appears on Your Website

## Features

✅ **Direct Links:** Both contact and footer sections link directly to your Instagram profile  
✅ **New Tab:** Links open in a new tab so users don't leave your site  
✅ **Secure:** Uses `rel="noopener noreferrer"` for security  
✅ **Hover Effects:** Interactive hover animations on Instagram buttons  
✅ **Mobile Friendly:** Works perfectly on all devices  

---

## How Users Can Find You

1. **On Contact Page:** Users can click the Instagram button in the "Follow Us" section
2. **On Footer:** Users can click the Instagram button at the bottom of every page
3. **Anywhere on Site:** The footer appears on all pages, making Instagram always accessible

---

## Customization Options

If you want to add more social media platforms, you can:

1. Add them to `config/site.config.ts` in the `social` object
2. Update `components/contact.tsx` to display additional social links
3. Update `components/footer.tsx` to include them in the footer
